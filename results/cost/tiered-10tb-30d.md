# Elasticsearch tiered setups vs Cartridge, 10 TB/day, 30 days searchable

Estimate only: the cost model as a calculator (matrix.price_row / system_totals; generated by `uv run cost-tiered`). Serverless is billed on ingested/retained GB, so it is the same $ for every dataset. Compute coefficients stay the http_logs measurements. "Cartridge" is the larger of the two measured Cartridge outputs per dataset.
